## Fact checks

Pass the relevant lines to image generation models as explicit constraints if needed, or validate generated image against this list. 

- Front bezels are not uniform on all sides: left, top, and right bezels are the same, while the bottom bezel is wider. Compare against the front view.

- Three buttons have different colors: AI/OK/Power button is grey, while Up and Down buttons are white

- The front bezel and the rear shell is light silver-grey, while all four edges are white.
- Glass touch cover over the screen. The screen itself has a matte, paper-like look.
- Porject screenshots have mono-color E-paper texture. No color, no backlight glow, no glossy LCD look.
